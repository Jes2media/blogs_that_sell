# SEO Content Plan: blogsthatsell.com

---

## 1) Executive Summary

This SEO content plan establishes a TOFU/MOFU/BOFU funnel architecture for blogsthatsell.com designed to capture organic search traffic, build topical authority around direct-response blogging, and systematically funnel readers toward email capture and course presale pages.

**Scale Target: 1,000+ indexed pages**

The strategy uses programmatic content patterns that multiply across niches, frameworks, and formats to achieve scale while maintaining quality and conversion focus. Core patterns include niche-specific strategy pages (100+ niches), framework breakdowns (30+ frameworks × multiple formats), and problem-aware content that captures high-intent searches.

### How Pillars Function

1. **Receive links** from TOFU and MOFU content (cluster model)

4. **Rank for head terms** that TOFU/MOFU cannot capture individually

### Specific Rules

**TOFU Posts Must:**
- [ ] Link to at least 1 relevant MOFU page
- [ ] Link to at least 1 Pillar page
- [ ] Include CTA to `/free-training` at end of post

**MOFU Posts Must:**
- [ ] Link to `/get-more-sales` (primary CTA section)
- [ ] Link to `/free-training` (mid-article CTA)
- [ ] Link to `/blog-sales-letter-templates` (when relevant)
- [ ] Link to parent Pillar page
- [ ] Include at least 1 contextual link to related MOFU

**BOFU Pages Must:**
- [ ] Link back to primary Pillar (`/blogs-that-sell`)
- [ ] Link to 1-2 relevant MOFU pages (supporting content)
- [ ] Not link to TOFU (preserve link equity)

**Pillar Pages Must:**
- [ ] Link to all BOFU pages
- [ ] Link to 3-5 supporting MOFU pages
- [ ] Receive links from all related TOFU/MOFU

### Anchor Text Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use natural, varied anchor text
- Include keyword variations (not exact match every time)
- Use descriptive phrases ("learn the complete system" vs "click here")
- Mix branded anchors ("Blogs That Sell system") with generic

**Don't:**
- Over-optimize with exact-match anchors
- Use the same anchor text repeatedly for one page
- Force awkward keyword insertions
- Use naked URLs as anchors

**Anchor Text Distribution Target:**
- 30% branded/natural ("the system", "free training")
- 30% partial match ("blog conversion strategies", "direct response framework")
- 20% exact match (use sparingly)
- 20% generic + descriptive ("learn more about this", "get the complete guide")

### Content Guidelines for Guru-Anchor Posts

**DO:**
- Mention the figure naturally, as context for a larger point
- Acknowledge their contributions while adding nuance
- Position yourself as someone who's tested their advice
- Extract principles, critique tactics
- Link to our framework posts where their methods apply

**DON'T:**
- Write fan content or summaries of their work
- Use their name as primary keyword (contextual only)
- Attack them personally—critique ideas, not people
- Pretend to have insider knowledge you don't have
- Over-reference (1-3 mentions per post, max)

### Internal Linking Strategy

Each guru-anchor post should link to:
- Related framework post (if applicable)
- `/blogs-that-sell` pillar
- `/free-training` CTA
- One related diagnostic post from the 30-post plan

### Search Intent Notes

People searching these figures are often:
- Looking for shortcuts/formulas
- Frustrated that advice isn't working
- Ready to buy if they find something credible
- Skeptical of hype, responsive to nuance

Write for the frustrated sophisticate, not the eager beginner.
