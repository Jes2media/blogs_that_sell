# SEO Content Plan: blogsthatsell.com

---

## 1) Executive Summary

This SEO content plan establishes a TOFU/MOFU/BOFU funnel architecture for blogsthatsell.com designed to capture organic search traffic, build topical authority around direct-response blogging, and systematically funnel readers toward email capture and course presale pages. The strategy prioritizes high-intent keywords at the bottom of funnel while scaling traffic through programmatic TOFU content that targets freelancers, consultants, coaches, and marketing professionals searching for blog-to-sales conversion strategies.

## 3) Pillar Pages

Pillar pages are comprehensive, authoritative resources that anchor topic clusters and pass link equity throughout the site.

| Pillar Page | Topic Coverage | Receives Links From | Passes Authority To |
|-------------|----------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| `/blog-that-sells` | Core philosophy of direct-response blogging | All TOFU + MOFU | `/the-system`, `/free-training` |
| `/start-here` | Beginner orientation + site navigation | Homepage, email welcome | All pillars, `/free-training` |
| `/blog-sales-letter-templates` | Template library + methodology | TOFU/MOFU template posts | `/the-system` |
| `/case-studies` | Proof + results documentation | MOFU strategy posts | `/the-system`, `/free-training` |

### How Pillars Function

1. **Receive links** from TOFU and MOFU content (cluster model)
2. **Build topical authority** through comprehensive coverage
3. **Pass authority** to BOFU money pages via strategic internal links
4. **Rank for head terms** that TOFU/MOFU cannot capture individually

## 4) MOFU Content Types (Workhorses)

## 7) Internal Linking Rules (Non-Negotiables)

### Minimum Link Requirements by Content Type

| Content Type | Links To BOFU | Links To Pillar | Links To MOFU | Links To TOFU |
|--------------|---------------|-----------------|---------------|---------------|
| TOFU | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| MOFU | 2-3 | 1 | 0-1 | 0 |
| Pillar | 2-3 | - | 2-3 | 0 |
| BOFU | 0 | 1 | 1-2 | 0 |

### Specific Rules

**TOFU Posts Must:**
- [ ] Link to at least 1 relevant MOFU page
- [ ] Link to at least 1 Pillar page
- [ ] Include CTA to `/free-training` at end of post

**MOFU Posts Must:**
- [ ] Link to `/the-system` (primary CTA section)
- [ ] Link to `/free-training` (mid-article CTA)
- [ ] Link to `/blog-sales-letter-templates` (when relevant)
- [ ] Link to parent Pillar page
- [ ] Include at least 1 contextual link to related MOFU

**BOFU Pages Must:**
- [ ] Link back to primary Pillar (`/blog-that-sells`)
- [ ] Link to 1-2 relevant MOFU pages (supporting content)
- [ ] Not link to TOFU (preserve link equity)

**Pillar Pages Must:**
- [ ] Link to all BOFU pages
- [ ] Link to 3-5 supporting MOFU pages
- [ ] Receive links from all related TOFU/MOFU

### Anchor Text Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use natural, varied anchor text
- Include keyword variations (not exact match every time)
- Use descriptive phrases ("learn the complete system" vs "click here")
- Mix branded anchors ("Blogs That Sell system") with generic

**Don't:**
- Over-optimize with exact-match anchors
- Use the same anchor text repeatedly for one page
- Force awkward keyword insertions
- Use naked URLs as anchors

**Anchor Text Distribution Target:**
- 30% branded/natural ("the system", "free training")
- 30% partial match ("blog conversion strategies", "direct response framework")
- 20% exact match (use sparingly)
- 20% generic + descriptive ("learn more about this", "get the complete guide")
