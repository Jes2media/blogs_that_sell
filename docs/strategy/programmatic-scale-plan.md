# BlogsThatSell – Programmatic Scale Plan (30,000-Foot View)

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines the long-term strategy for programmatic content on BlogsThatSell.

Its purpose is to:
- Keep programmatic pages aligned with rankings-first goals
- Prevent drift into generic or diluted content
- Clearly separate programmatic content from long-form editorial content
- Provide guardrails for scaling decisions as the site grows

Claude should treat this document as **authoritative strategy**, not a prompt to optimize or rewrite.

---

## Core Goal

The goal of programmatic content on BlogsThatSell is:

**To rank for high-intent, buyer-adjacent copywriting queries at scale
using structured, opinionated, non-generic pages.**

This content exists to:
- Capture demand
- Build topical authority
- Feed internal links to higher-value pages
- Identify winners to later upgrade into long-form content

Programmatic content is not intended to replace or mimic long-form essays.

---

## Current Phase (Locked)

**Phase:** Validation & Foundation
**Batch Size:** 10 articles at a time
**Initial Cap:** 100 total programmatic pages

No expansion beyond 100 pages should occur until:
- Search Console data is reviewed
- Impression patterns are identified
- Winners and weak clusters are assessed

---

## Primary Scaling Axis (Current)

During the first 100 pages, scaling is limited to:

**[Copywriting Type] × [Business Type] × Tips**

Examples:
- Email copywriting tips for local service businesses
- Landing page copywriting tips for SaaS
- Sales page copywriting tips for coaches

No additional intent types (mistakes, templates, examples, etc.) are added during Phase 1.

---

## Why This Axis Was Chosen

This axis was selected because it:
- Has real, existing search demand
- Is buyer-adjacent without being salesy
- Scales cleanly
- Allows niche-specific examples
- Avoids guru dependency as a primary keyword

---

## Quality Bar (Non-Negotiable)

All programmatic pages must:
- Be specific to the business type
- Include original, niche-aware examples
- Avoid generic "best practices" language
- Avoid fabricated stats, studies, or quotes
- Avoid endorsement or affiliation implications

The goal is **useful, rankable content**, not volume for its own sake.

---

## Separation of Content Types (Critical)

Programmatic content:
- Uses its own spec and template
- Lives in a dedicated folder
- Is structured, repeatable, and concise
- Prioritizes clarity over personality

Long-form content:
- Retains its existing tone and voice
- Is written manually or with higher creative freedom
- Is not constrained by programmatic templates
- Serves authority, narrative, and brand positioning

These two content types should **never be merged or averaged**.

---

## Review & Decision Point

At approximately **100 programmatic pages**, the strategy will be reassessed.

Possible next steps include:
- Expanding business types
- Adding new intent types (mistakes, examples, templates)
- Upgrading top-performing pages into long-form
- Pruning or consolidating weak clusters

No assumptions are made beyond this point.

Decisions should be data-driven, not speculative.

---

## Guiding Principle

Scale follows signal.

If something ranks, it earns expansion.
If something does not, it does not get more pages.

This site grows by **proof, not optimism**.
