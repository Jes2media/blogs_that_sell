# Project: Blogs That Sell

## Overview
A direct-response content marketing site teaching freelancers, consultants, and marketers how to write blog posts that convert. Monetized via course (videos + PDFs), templates, and email marketing products.

## Target Audience
- Freelancers, consultants, coaches
- Marketing directors, content marketers
- People influenced by Dan Kennedy, Frank Kern, Russell Brunson

## Content Guidelines
1. All blog content should follow the SEO plan's funnel structure
2. Use the TOFU or MOFU page brief templates for new posts
3. Follow internal linking rules (TOFU → MOFU + Pillar, MOFU → BOFU)
4. Apply direct-response copywriting principles from the skills
