**Send: Day 2**

**Subject:** The sales letter that changed everything

**Body:**

Yesterday I told you I was ready to quit blogging.

Here's what happened instead.

I was cleaning out my office and found an old book—a collection of famous sales letters from direct-response legends like Gary Halbert and Dan Kennedy.

I started reading. Not to apply it to blogging—just out of curiosity.

And then it hit me.

These sales letters were doing everything my blog posts weren't:

• They opened with problems, not features
• They agitated pain before presenting solutions
• They handled objections before readers could raise them
• They made a clear, compelling call to action
• Every sentence moved toward a specific goal

My blog posts? They educated. They informed. They provided "value."

But they didn't *move* anyone.

That's when I had the realization:

**A blog post can do everything a sales letter does—while still ranking on Google.**

It's not about being "salesy." It's about being *intentional*.

Instead of writing to inform, write to transform.

Instead of hoping readers convert, engineer the path to conversion.

I started applying direct-response principles to my content:

• Buyer-intent keywords instead of vanity keywords
• Problem-first openings instead of generic intros
• Strategic CTAs throughout, not just at the end
• Proof and specifics instead of vague claims

The results weren't immediate. But within a few months, everything changed.

Posts that used to get ignored started generating leads. Traffic that used to bounce started converting. My blog became a sales asset instead of a content graveyard.

## Kit Setup Instructions

1. **Create the sequence:**
   - Go to Automate → Visual Automations → New Automation
   - Trigger: "Joins a form" → Select your free training form

2. **Add emails:**
   - Add Email step for each email above
   - Set delays between emails (0, 1 day, 1 day, 1 day, 1 day)

3. **Set up the PDF delivery:**
   - Upload the PDF to Kit or host on your site
   - Add the download link to Email 1

4. **Test the sequence:**
   - Add yourself as a subscriber
   - Verify all emails send correctly
