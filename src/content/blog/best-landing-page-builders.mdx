![Landing page builders comparison](/images/blog-landing-page-tools-hero.png)

Your blog attracts visitors. A landing page converts them into leads.

The right landing page builder makes it easy to create high-converting opt-in pages without coding or design skills. The wrong one adds friction, costs too much, or limits what you can do.

Here's a practical review of the best options for different needs and budgets.

## What to Look For

Before comparing tools, understand what matters:

**Ease of use:** Can you create pages without a developer?

**Templates:** Does it include conversion-focused templates?

**Speed:** How fast do pages load? Slow pages kill conversions.

**Integration:** Does it connect with your email platform?

**A/B testing:** Can you test variations to improve performance?

**Pricing:** Does the cost make sense for your volume?

## All-in-One Marketing Platforms

These tools include landing pages as part of a broader marketing suite.

### ConvertKit

**Best for:** Creators and bloggers who want simplicity

ConvertKit's landing page builder is designed for creators—simple, fast, and integrated with its email platform.

**Strengths:**
- Simple and fast to build
- Built into email platform
- Good templates for creators
- Free tier includes landing pages
- Fast page load times

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited customization
- Basic A/B testing
- Less sophisticated than dedicated tools

**Best use case:** Bloggers and creators who want landing pages that just work without fuss.

**Cost:** Free tier available, paid plans from $9/month

### Mailchimp

**Best for:** Small businesses already using Mailchimp

If you're already in Mailchimp for email, their landing pages keep everything in one place.

**Strengths:**
- Integrated with Mailchimp email
- Simple drag-and-drop builder
- Good templates
- Free tier available

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited compared to dedicated tools
- Basic analytics
- Less flexible than alternatives

**Best use case:** Existing Mailchimp users who want simple landing pages.

**Cost:** Included in Mailchimp plans (free tier available)

### HubSpot

**Best for:** Businesses wanting a full marketing platform

HubSpot's landing pages are part of their comprehensive marketing suite—CRM, email, analytics, and more.

**Strengths:**
- Full marketing platform integration
- Sophisticated personalization
- Strong analytics
- A/B testing included
- CRM integration

**Weaknesses:**
- Expensive for full features
- Complexity if you only need landing pages
- Learning curve

**Best use case:** Businesses wanting integrated marketing automation with landing pages.

**Cost:** Free tools available, paid plans from $45/month

## Dedicated Landing Page Builders

These tools specialize in landing pages with more features and flexibility.

### Leadpages

**Best for:** Small businesses focused on lead generation

Leadpages is purpose-built for lead generation with conversion-focused templates and easy setup.

**Strengths:**
- Conversion-focused templates
- Easy drag-and-drop builder
- Built-in A/B testing
- Good integration options
- Alert bars and pop-ups included

**Weaknesses:**
- Monthly cost adds up
- Some templates feel dated
- Less flexible than some competitors

**Best use case:** Small businesses and solopreneurs who need landing pages that convert without complexity.

**Cost:** Starting at $37/month

### Unbounce

**Best for:** Marketers who want optimization features

Unbounce offers sophisticated landing page building with AI-powered optimization and advanced testing.

**Strengths:**
- Smart Traffic (AI optimization)
- Advanced A/B testing
- Dynamic text replacement
- Pop-ups and sticky bars
- Fast page speeds

**Weaknesses:**
- Higher price point
- More complex than simpler tools
- Some features require higher tiers

**Best use case:** Marketers running significant traffic who want to optimize conversion rates.

**Cost:** Starting at $74/month

### Instapage

**Best for:** Teams and agencies needing collaboration

Instapage emphasizes collaboration, personalization, and enterprise features.

**Strengths:**
- Team collaboration features
- 1:1 ad-to-page personalization
- Heatmaps included
- Fast page loading
- Advanced analytics

**Weaknesses:**
- Expensive
- Enterprise focus may be overkill
- Complex for simple needs

**Best use case:** Marketing teams and agencies managing multiple landing pages at scale.

**Cost:** Starting at $79/month

### ClickFunnels

**Best for:** Sales funnels beyond just landing pages

ClickFunnels builds complete sales funnels, not just landing pages—opt-in pages, sales pages, order forms, upsells.

**Strengths:**
- Complete funnel building
- Templates for full customer journeys
- Training and community included
- Affiliate management
- Checkout functionality built in

**Weaknesses:**
- Expensive
- Can be overwhelming
- Overkill if you only need landing pages
- Some templates feel aggressive

**Best use case:** Course creators and info-product sellers who want complete sales funnels.

**Cost:** Starting at $97/month

## Website Builder Add-Ons

If you already have a website, these tools add landing page functionality.

### Elementor (WordPress)

**Best for:** WordPress users wanting full control

Elementor is a WordPress page builder that can create any page type, including landing pages.

**Strengths:**
- Full design control
- One-time or low annual cost
- Huge template library
- Popup builder included (Pro)
- WordPress integration

**Weaknesses:**
- WordPress required
- Can slow down sites if misused
- Learning curve
- Pro features require paid version

**Best use case:** WordPress users who want landing page capability without additional monthly costs.

**Cost:** Free version available, Pro from $59/year

### Webflow

**Best for:** Designers who want code-free flexibility

Webflow offers designer-level control without coding—powerful but with a learning curve.

**Strengths:**
- Designer-level flexibility
- Clean, fast code output
- CMS capabilities
- Hosting included

**Weaknesses:**
- Steep learning curve
- More complex than drag-and-drop
- Monthly cost
- Overkill for simple pages

**Best use case:** Designers or design-conscious businesses wanting pixel-perfect landing pages.

**Cost:** Starting at $14/month for site plans

### Carrd

**Best for:** Simple, single-page sites

Carrd creates simple one-page sites perfect for basic landing pages and link-in-bio pages.

**Strengths:**
- Extremely simple
- Very affordable
- Fast pages
- Good for single-purpose pages

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited functionality
- Basic compared to alternatives
- No A/B testing
- Limited forms

**Best use case:** Simple lead capture pages, link-in-bio pages, minimal MVP landing pages.

**Cost:** Free tier, Pro from $9/year

## Free Options

### Mailchimp Landing Pages

Free landing pages tied to Mailchimp's free email tier. Limited but functional.

### ConvertKit Landing Pages

Free landing pages with ConvertKit's free tier. Simple but effective for creators.

### Carrd (Free Tier)

Simple one-page sites with Carrd branding. Good for testing concepts.

### HubSpot Free Tools

Basic landing pages with HubSpot's free CRM tier. Limited customization but integrated.

## How to Choose

### Choose an all-in-one platform when:
- You need email + landing pages together
- Simplicity matters more than features
- You're just starting out
- Budget is limited

### Choose a dedicated landing page builder when:
- Landing page performance is critical
- You need A/B testing capabilities
- You're running significant traffic
- Conversion optimization is a priority

### Choose a website builder add-on when:
- You already have a WordPress/Webflow site
- You want to avoid additional monthly costs
- You need design flexibility
- You're comfortable with more complexity

## What Actually Matters for Conversion

The tool matters less than what you do with it.

### Speed
Slow pages lose visitors. Whatever tool you use, optimize for fast loading.

### Clear Value Proposition
Your headline should immediately communicate what visitors get.

### Single Focus
One page, one goal. Remove distractions and competing links.

### Strong CTA
Make the action obvious and the button prominent.

### Social Proof
Testimonials, logos, or subscriber counts build trust.

### Mobile Experience
Most traffic is mobile. Your page must work perfectly on phones.

See [how to write CTAs that convert](/blog/how-to-write-ctas-that-convert) for more on the copy side.

## My Recommendation

**For most bloggers and solopreneurs:**
- Start with ConvertKit or Mailchimp if you use them for email
- Or use Leadpages if you want dedicated functionality
- Carrd works for ultra-simple pages

**For WordPress users:**
- Elementor Pro gives you flexibility without monthly landing page costs

**For serious optimization:**
- Unbounce if you're running significant traffic and want to optimize

**Don't over-tool.** A simple landing page that loads fast and communicates clearly will outperform a sophisticated page that's slow or confusing.

## Your Next Step

If you don't have a landing page for your lead magnet:
1. Pick the simplest tool that integrates with your email platform
2. Create one landing page for your primary lead magnet
3. Focus on clear headline, obvious CTA, fast loading
4. Drive traffic to it and measure conversion rate

If you have landing pages:
1. Check load times (aim for under 3 seconds)
2. Review conversion rates—under 20% likely has room to improve
3. Consider A/B testing headlines or CTAs

The tool enables the work. The work is creating pages that convert.
